# Product brief

## Product

GolfSG is a mobile-friendly, offline-first golf putting analysis tool. It turns simple practice or round inputs into strokes-gained feedback that helps a golfer decide what to practice next.

## Problem

Golfers often know their total putts but not where those putts came from. A total score does not clearly distinguish poor distance control, missed short putts, three-putts, or unusually difficult starting positions. GolfSG is intended to make that feedback quick enough to use during practice and structured enough to review later.

## Primary users

### The self-coached golfer

Wants a simple record of practice and round performance without needing a cloud account, spreadsheet, or complex statistics setup.

### The data-oriented golfer

Wants strokes-gained results, distance-specific trends, comparisons, and a history that can support deliberate practice.

### The coach-supported golfer

Wants to export a useful summary and discuss concrete weaknesses with a coach.

## Product principles

- **Fast on the course:** common inputs should require as few taps as possible.
- **Explainable:** results should show what was measured and how the score was derived.
- **Offline-first:** recording a round should not depend on connectivity or an account.
- **Honest about uncertainty:** baseline assumptions and incomplete data should be visible.
- **Actionable:** statistics should lead to a practice decision, not just another number.
- **Respectful of user data:** local history should remain under the player's control.

## Current product scope

The alpha focuses on putting rounds, guided entry, training games, benchmarks, local history, and basic insights. Approach and around-the-green tracking are included in the architecture but are not yet considered stable product areas.

## Non-goals for the alpha

- Replacing official scoring, handicap, or tournament systems.
- Providing a complete golf-course GPS experience.
- Requiring a social network, account, or cloud service.
- Presenting the reference baseline as a personalized truth for every golfer.

## Measures of progress

Until product analytics are deliberately introduced, progress should be assessed through:

- A new user completing a short putting session without instructions.
- A player returning to history and understanding what to practice next.
- Successful export and recovery of saved history.
- Reliable behavior across Windows and at least one real mobile device.
- Feedback showing that entry is fast enough during a real practice session.
