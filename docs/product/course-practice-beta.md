# Course practice rounds (beta)

Enable beta features in Settings, then open **Banerunde på billede · Beta** on the start screen. The menu offers **Spil bane** to choose a saved course and **Opsæt ny bane** to create one. An unfinished round can be resumed or explicitly discarded from the menu. Each position has a configured number of independent attempts from the same start towards the same target; this is not full golf-hole play until holed.

## Set up a round

1. Choose **Opsæt ny bane** in the menu, enter a course name and press **Fortsæt**. Setup opens directly on the picture, ready to tap the first starting point. The course name appears in the page title and can be changed near **Gem bane**; the beta explanation is also below the shot editor. **Annuller** returns without creating a course. The same name-first flow applies when creating a course from the empty saved-course list. To adjust an existing course, choose **Spil bane**, then **Rediger bane** on its card. New courses use the bundled facility picture, which is available offline. Users cannot select or upload custom course pictures.
2. Calibrate the picture: press **Kalibrer målestok**, place two points with a known distance, press the button again, and enter that distance in metres. The supplied picture starts with an approximate scale based on the user's roughly 110 m reference, not verified map accuracy.
3. Choose **Start**, tap the starting position, then tap the target. A compact summary below the picture shows the whole-metre distance, labelled **Estimeret afstand** or **Målt afstand** when a measured override is used. It updates while dragging and when selecting a saved shot, and disappears for an incomplete shot or calibration. Drag either marker to adjust it, or drag the connecting line to move both positions together. Green marks the start and orange marks the target. Zoom can help with placement.
4. Set **Antal slag** to a whole number from 1 to 100 (default 1), then choose the starting lie and SG category. For example, 5 means playing five balls from that same position, each with its own outcome and SG. A course supports up to 100 positions and 1,000 attempts in total. Green starts use Putting; tee shots use Approach. Choose Around Green for chips and greenside bunker shots; category is explicit because distance to the green edge cannot be inferred from a picture.
5. Optionally enter a measured shot distance to override the image estimate. Name the shot and press **Gem slag og fortsæt**. This single action saves or updates the shot, clears the editor, dismisses the keyboard and returns to the picture, ready for the next starting point. Invalid input keeps the pending shot and its positions intact so it can be corrected. Saved shots remain numbered on the picture: drag a marker to adjust an endpoint, or drag its line to move the whole shot. Distance updates while dragging. Moving an endpoint clears an old measured-distance override; moving the whole shot preserves it. Save the course to keep these changes. Select a shot in the list to change its name, lie, category or measured distance; **Gem slag og fortsæt** updates that shot in its original position in the list.
6. Press **Gem bane** to save the layout and return. If an unfinished shot remains after at least one saved shot, you can explicitly discard that draft and save the existing layout, or choose **Tilbage** to keep editing; no shot is silently discarded. Finish calibration before saving. Choose **Spil bane** in the menu, then **Spil bane** on the saved course's card to start the round directly. Courses can be reused. Starting a round freezes the layout, attempt counts, lie, distance overrides and calibration for that session. Existing saved courses without an attempt count retain one attempt per position.

Planned shot distances show whole metres in setup, play and saved results: 10.4 m shows as 10 m, and 10.5 m shows as 11 m. The original geometry and measurements retain their precision for calibration and SG. Editable measurements and actual finishing distances can still contain decimals.

## Play

1. Starting or resuming a course opens **Slagoversigt**, with all positions numbered on the course picture. Tap a position on the picture or a number below it to see its name, whole-metre distance, starting lie, category and progress, such as **2/5 slag spillet**. Before recording its first result, use **Antal slag − / +** above **Spil slag** to adjust the count for this round; the saved course's defaults remain unchanged. Press **Spil slag** to choose a position or **Fortsæt slag** to continue a partly played position. Remaining positions can be played in any order. Completed positions show their total SG and cannot be selected again.
2. Go to the shown hitting position and play the configured shots towards the target, then register each result in turn. **Slag N af M** shows the attempt being entered. **Slagoversigt** returns to the picture and position selector without losing current input. Choose **Fortsæt slag** to continue that same task. Switching to a different shot with unregistered input requires confirming that this input may be cleared.
3. Enter the actual remaining distance to that target in metres and the ending lie, or select **I hul**. Add any penalty strokes.
4. Press **Gem og næste slag** in the fixed bottom bar. It stays outside the scrolling form, with space reserved above the keyboard. The attempt is saved, outcome input clears, and the counter advances. While more attempts remain at this position, the form stays at your scroll position so you can enter the next result. After its last attempt, the next remaining position's picture opens; use **Slagoversigt** to choose another one at any time. Invalid input or a save failure keeps the current attempt and displays the error beside the button. On the final attempt of the round, **Gem sidste slag** saves it and opens the completed overview. Each attempt starts from the original position, not where the previous ball finished. The play page previews the latest ten results; the completed round retains every result.
5. Use **Fortryd seneste resultat** to correct the latest result. The last task actually played reopens with its input restored, even if shots were selected out of order. If current unregistered input would be cleared, confirm or cancel first. Undo can be repeated; later undone results then need to be entered again.
6. **Gem og luk** preserves the round, chosen shot and current input. Open the beta course menu and press **Fortsæt træningsrunde** to resume through the overview. Starting another practice round requires resuming or explicitly discarding the active one first; the saved-course list keeps new-round buttons disabled while a round is active.
7. After all configured attempts are registered, press **Gem rundens resultat** in the fixed bottom bar. Results appear on Start and in History under **Banetræning · Beta**, with each outcome matched to its position and attempt in actual play order. Choosing positions during play preserves the reusable course's original layout and order.

SG is expected strokes at the start minus expected strokes at the finish, minus the single shot and penalties. Holed shots have zero expected finish strokes. This uses the application's existing approximate putting, approach and short-game baselines. Long tee-shot/off-the-tee scoring and full hole play are outside this beta flow.

Practice rounds are excluded from normal statistics and Start insights, since single-shot attempts are not comparable to full-hole scores or putting rounds. History contains their individual outcomes, course name, layout and scale. The ordinary round export/import includes these result details, but not local course-image files or the saved-course library. Results remain readable without their original image; transferring/recovering the editable course library needs a separate future export flow.

## Verification

Automated coverage checks image aspect ratios, measured overrides, endpoint and whole-shot movement at image boundaries, attempt-count limits, immutable round setup, repeated attempts, partial-position selection and persistence, stale input/result protection, SG outcomes and penalties, duplicate submission, undo in actual play order, failed-save recovery, idempotent archiving, backup recovery, legacy data migration, future-schema preservation and round export/import alignment. Physical-device checks remain necessary for the course-name prompt, shot overview and draft-switch confirmation, offline picture rendering, calibration, tap/drag versus scrolling, large text and lifecycle recovery.
